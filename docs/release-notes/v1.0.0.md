# Family Finance Tracker v1.0.0

The first release: a free, self-hosted, read-only finance tracker for one US Household. It pulls Transactions from the Household's banks, cards, and loans through Plaid, separates real Spending from money that moves between the Household's own Accounts, and gives analysis and AI advice with no manual upkeep.

## What it does

- **Bank sync.** Transactions, balances and loans come in through Plaid, on a schedule.
- **Money movement.** Transfers, Refunds and Reimbursements between the Household's own Accounts are linked, so they do not count as Spending or Income.
- **Categories.** The app sets each Transaction's Category. The Review list holds the few it is not sure of.
- **Budgets, Benchmarks and Goals.** Draft Budgets from your own history, and compare Spending with public figures for similar Households.
- **Recurring charges and Alerts.** The app finds subscriptions and bills, and sends push Alerts for unusual charges.
- **Reports.** Four reports: Spending over time, Cash flow, Balances and debt, and Merchants and Recurring charges. Tap a bar to see its Transactions.
- **Amazon orders.** Upload Amazon's order history file. The app links each shipment's Line items to the card Transaction that paid for it, and splits the Transaction by Category. The app never signs in to Amazon.
- **AI.** Chat, Insight cards and a Monthly review, with the AI provider you choose.
- **Backups.** Automatic backups, restore, and data export.

## Install

Follow the [setup guide](https://github.com/therecluse26/family-finance-tracker/blob/v1.0.0/docs/self-hosting.md). It covers Docker Compose, HTTPS for phones and other devices, a rented server or a platform service, Plaid sign-up, backups and restore, updates, and the Master key. The [README quick start](https://github.com/therecluse26/family-finance-tracker/blob/v1.0.0/README.md#quick-start) runs the app on one computer in a few commands.

## The image

The image is for amd64 and arm64, under three names:

- `ghcr.io/therecluse26/family-finance-tracker:1.0.0`
- `ghcr.io/therecluse26/family-finance-tracker:1`, which `compose.yaml` uses, so it takes every 1.x release
- `ghcr.io/therecluse26/family-finance-tracker:latest`

## Before you start

- Save the Master key that the setup wizard shows in a password manager. Without it, a backup restores with every Connection lost.
- Plaid Items are limited for life. Never delete `data/` to start over: restore a backup instead.

The [v1 spec](https://github.com/therecluse26/family-finance-tracker/blob/v1.0.0/docs/spec/README.md) holds every rule, and [Data sources](https://github.com/therecluse26/family-finance-tracker/blob/v1.0.0/docs/data-sources.md) lists the public data the Benchmarks are built from.
